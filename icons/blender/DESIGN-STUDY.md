# Soft depth: the revised icon direction

Historical review notes from October 5. The current expanded glass family and
its material corrections are documented in [GLASS-CONTRACT.md](GLASS-CONTRACT.md).

Observed from Parakeet's [Cordelia](https://parakeet.co/app-icons/cordelia/),
[Linkmate](https://parakeet.co/app-icons/linkmate/), and
[Mezzanine](https://parakeet.co/app-icons/mezzanine/v2/) on October 5, 2026.
These are visual observations, not claims about Parakeet's production tools.
No Parakeet artwork is copied into the project.

## What makes dimensional artwork remain graphically simple

- The view is almost frontal. Perspective never obscures the identifying shape.
- Most of the depth appears at boundaries: a soft edge highlight, a nearby
  contact shadow, or one overlapping translucent layer.
- Large smooth color transitions do more work than small surface details.
- Corners and silhouettes have continuous, gentle curvature.
- Highlights are wide and restrained. The whole object is not covered in hard
  chrome reflections or a thick black outline.
- Color is concentrated in a clear focal shape. Generous quiet space surrounds
  it. Each app can have its own palette while sharing lighting and optical size.
- At small sizes, shape and color survive; fine texture quietly disappears.

## Corrections to the rejected drafts

The first vector set was too uniform and schematic. The first Blender material
study went too far toward rough metal, thick extrusion and prominent rims.
Both are rejected directions; neither is the active desktop icon theme.

The revised Blender study uses shallow geometry, softened illumination,
near-frontal orthographic cameras, satin materials and very fine surface relief.
Major-app identity stays intact. Brave uses its original vector lion geometry;
Ghostty retains the ghost and terminal prompt; Darktable retains its colored
aperture. Inkscape remains useful for exact vector silhouettes and export sheets.

## Review gates before replacement

1. Judge the silhouette at 24, 32, 48 and 64px on light and near-black surfaces.
2. Keep optical weight consistent; do not stretch logos into the same shape.
3. Inspect 768px renders for contour artifacts, banding, grain and overexposure.
4. Compare material softness against the references, not only against the prior
   draft. A technically valid render is not a design-quality pass.
5. Review a few representative apps before propagating the treatment to the
   remaining set. Do not mass-apply an unreviewed direction again.

The prior Conflux icon theme has been restored. These three Blender scenes are
review studies; full icon coverage, final polish and installation are pending.
